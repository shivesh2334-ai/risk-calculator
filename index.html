Protocol Equations, Clinical Calibration & Source References
  • LAI 2023 Consensus IV: Puri R, Bansal M, Mehta V, Duell PB, Wong ND, et al. Lipid Association of India 2023 update on cardiovascular risk assessment and lipid management in Indian patients: Consensus statement IV. J Clin Lipidol. 2024;18(3):e351–e373 (DOI). Implements Figures 1–5, Tables 1–2 (MESA/CARDIA 75th-percentile CACS thresholds extrapolated for Indians), and Table 3 lipid targets.
  • ASCVD Protocol — ACC/AHA PCE (2013): Goff DC Jr, Lloyd-Jones DM, et al. Circulation. 2014;129:S49–S73. Sex- and race-specific 10-year hard ASCVD Cox models (ages 40–79) plus ACC/AHA legacy lifetime risk strata (ages 20–59).
  • ASCVD Protocol — AHA PREVENT (2024): Khan SS, Matsushita K, Sang Y, et al. Circulation. 2024;149(6):430–449. Race-free 10-year (ages 30–79) and 30-year (ages 30–59) Base and Enhanced (HbA1c, UACR, SDI, Full) models for ASCVD, Total CVD, Heart Failure, CHD, and Stroke.
  • QRISK3 Protocol (2017): Hippisley-Cox J, Coupland C, Brindle P. BMJ. 2017;357:j2099. Open-source 22-variable sex-specific Cox proportional hazards algorithm (ages 25–84) with fractional polynomials and age interactions, alongside optional QRISK3-lifetime entry.
