Implementation and validation plan
  1. Use LAI Consensus IV Figures 1–2 for categorical stratification. Apply the highest supported tier and show the triggering findings. Preserve missing inputs and unclassified source boundaries for review.
  2. Calculate the legacy ACC risk-factor reference locally. Record QRISK3-lifetime and PREVENT results from their official tools with outcome, model and horizon. ACC legacy lifetime is a US age-50 risk-factor profile reference. ACC/AHA 2026 uses PREVENT-ASCVD for 10-year and, where eligible, 30-year estimates. QRISK3-lifetime includes a selectable end age.
  3. Before clinical rollout, have two clinicians adjudicate borderline and overlapping rules, compare reference entries against official calculators and audit multiple treatment and imaging scenarios.
  4. To develop a numeric India-specific lifetime probability, use longitudinal Indian cohorts with adjudicated first ASCVD events and competing non-ASCVD death. Prespecify horizons and eligible ages, account for censoring and competing death, validate across Indian regions and sexes, and report calibration, discrimination and clinical utility before deployment.
